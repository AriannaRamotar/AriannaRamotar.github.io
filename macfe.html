01 / 2023–2024

EV5 — Building the Foundations

My first full competition season as Team Principal marked an important step in developing both my leadership and technical understanding of the team. Alongside operations, budgeting, and coordination, I became actively involved in the manufacturing process behind the vehicle.

  • Chassis Mould Manufacturing: Assisted with manufacturing the mould used in the carbon fibre chassis production process.
  • Composite Manufacturing: Contributed to carbon fibre cutting, layup, and infusion for the chassis.
  • Leadership & Operations: Supported organizational planning, budgeting, and coordination across technical and business functions.
  • Technical Collaboration: Participated in design reviews, including considerations related to Formula SAE rules compliance.

02 / 2024–2025

EV6 — Strengthening the Process

Building on the previous season, I continued balancing organizational leadership with hands-on involvement in chassis manufacturing and technical collaboration.

  • Chassis Manufacturing: Assisted with carbon fibre cutting, layup, and infusion throughout the manufacturing process.
  • Design Reviews: Participated in technical reviews to help assess rules compliance for specific components and design decisions.
  • Budgeting & Resources: Contributed to financial and resource considerations supporting team priorities.
  • Project Coordination: Remained actively involved in workshop activities, helping support manufacturing progress and project deadlines.

03 / 2025–2026

EV7 — A Year of Milestones

My final season as Team Principal brought together organizational growth, technical collaboration, hands-on manufacturing, major events, and the transition to a new leadership team.

  • Chassis Manufacturing: Continued assisting with carbon fibre cutting, layup, and infusion.
  • Design Reviews & Compliance: Participated in reviews focused on component requirements and Formula SAE rules compliance.
  • EV7 Reveal: Supported the March 2026 vehicle reveal, attended by more than 150 people.
  • Formula SAE Michigan: Supported team preparations and competition activities in June 2026.
  • Organizational Growth: Team membership reached 181 students, with 55 sponsors in 2026.
  • Leadership Transition: Concluded my Team Principal tenure in July 2026.
